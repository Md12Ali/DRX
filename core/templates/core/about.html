How it works

Simple booking. Clear progress. Fair rules.

DRX is my driving school in East London. You book and pay online, I pick you up and teach the lesson, and your progress is recorded against the same skills the driving test checks.

1. Create an account

You need an account so we can confirm your bookings, take payment and keep your progress safe. Add your mobile number and pick-up address once and we'll fill them in on every booking.

2. Book a lesson

  • Lessons cost {{ hourly_rate_display }} an hour. Choose 1 hour or 2 hours.
  • Pick-ups are available in {{ covered_list }}.
  • Pay by card, or use hours from a lesson package, which saves you money.
  • Lessons start on the hour or half past, at least 12 hours from now and up to 8 weeks ahead.
  • We hold your chosen time for 35 minutes while you pay. If you don't pay, the slot is released.

3. Learn and track your progress

After each lesson I rate the skills you covered on a five-step scale:

  1. Introduced: you've been shown how it works.
  2. Under full instruction: you can do it with step-by-step help.
  3. Prompted: you need the occasional reminder.
  4. Seldom prompted: you rarely need help.
  5. Independent: you do it safely on your own, every time.

You're test-ready when your overall score is at least 85% and every skill is at "Seldom prompted" or above.

Cancellations and refunds

  • Reschedule or cancel online up to 24 hours before a lesson. Card payments are refunded in full; package hours go back into your balance.
  • Inside 24 hours, please call me on {{ contact_phone }}. Late cancellations may not be refunded.
  • If I have to cancel, you always get your money or hours back.

Payments and your data

Payments are handled by Stripe. DRX never sees or stores your card details. You can delete your account at any time from "My details", which removes your bookings and progress.

Contact me

Call or text {{ contact_phone }} with any question about lessons, prices or pick-ups. If I'm driving, I'll call you back.
