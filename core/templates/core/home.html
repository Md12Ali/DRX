Questions

Good to know

Do I need a provisional licence?

Yes. You need a valid provisional licence before your first lesson. You can apply on GOV.UK from age 15 years and 9 months, and drive from 17.

Where do lessons start?

Your instructor picks you up from the address you give when booking. You can change it for each lesson.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or reschedule in your account up to 24 hours before the lesson for a full refund. After that, please call your instructor.

How many lessons will I need?

Everyone is different. Your test-ready score shows how close you are, and your instructor will tell you when to book your test.
