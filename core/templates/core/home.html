Questions

Good to know

Do I need a provisional licence?

Yes. You need a valid provisional licence before your first lesson. You can apply on GOV.UK from age 15 years and 9 months, and drive from 17.

How do packages work?

Buy a package and the hours go into your account. When you book, choose "Use package hours" instead of paying by card. If you cancel in time, the hours go back into your balance.

Where do lessons start?

I pick you up from any address in {{ covered_list }}. You can change the pick-up for each lesson.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or reschedule in your account up to 24 hours before the lesson. Card payments are refunded and package hours are returned. Inside 24 hours, please call me.
